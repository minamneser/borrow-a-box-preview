Ask us a question ↗
What is Borrow A Box?+

Borrow A Box is a reusable moving bin rental service designed to make moving easier, faster, and more sustainable. Instead of buying cardboard boxes, you rent durable plastic moving totes that are delivered to your door and picked up when you’re finished.

How does Borrow A Box work?+
  1. Select your tote package or quantity.
  2. We deliver clean, ready-to-use moving bins to your home or business.
  3. Pack your belongings — no tape or assembly needed.
  4. Move into your new space.
  5. We pick up the empty totes once you’re done unpacking.

Simple, convenient, and stress-free.

How long is the rental period?+

Seven calendar days are included. Add up to 28 extra days at $25 per day, with each seven-day block capped at $100. For example, 4–7 extra days cost $100 and 8 extra days cost $125. The pickup date and extension charge update in the booking preview.

What are the measurements of the bins?+

Approximate measurements are: 22″ (Depth) × 15.5″ (Width) × 12.9″ (Height).

Are the bins clean?+

Yes! Every bin is cleaned and sanitized between customers to ensure a safe and hygienic moving experience. Because our bins are reusable and eco-friendly, you may notice minor scuffs or marks — just signs they’ve helped other families move sustainably before you!

Do you deliver and pick up the bins?+

Choose separate delivery and pickup addresses and two-hour windows. Delivery and collection are included only in an approved standard service zone. Any additional charge is confirmed before payment. Preview windows are examples, not reserved availability.

What areas do you serve?+

Based in Central New Jersey, Borrow A Box delivers reusable moving boxes throughout New Jersey, with extended delivery available across the tri-state area for an additional fee. Subject to address and scheduling availability. Both ZIP codes are checked and additional charges are confirmed before payment.

What if my moving dates change?+

Contact us as soon as possible to check a new delivery or pickup date and equipment availability. The live agreement’s cancellation notice is separate from the booking cutoff: cancellations or changes at least 48 hours before delivery qualify for a full refund; later cancellations may incur a $25 restocking fee. Review the final accepted agreement.

How far ahead should I book?+

Book at least 48 hours before the start of your delivery window, using New Jersey time. Moving sooner? Email us to check short-notice availability. Urgent requests are subject to confirmation.

Can I add dollies or packing supplies?+

Yes. Add optional dollies at $25 each, subject to availability. Fragile kit contents and pricing, and other supply prices, are still being finalized; request those items in your quote without assuming a price.

When will I know the final total?+

The booking preview itemizes your package, extensions and priced extras. Both locations, delivery and collection charges, unpriced supplies and applicable tax must be confirmed in a final quote before payment. No payment or reservation is made in this preview.

Moving across town—or a little further?

Based in Central New Jersey, Borrow A Box delivers reusable moving boxes throughout New Jersey, with extended delivery available across the tri-state area for an additional fee. Subject to both addresses and scheduling availability. Any delivery or collection charge is confirmed before payment.
