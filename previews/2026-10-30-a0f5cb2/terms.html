Preview terms

This page explains the proposed booking flow. It does not change existing contracts or confirm an order. Bookings made on the original website follow the live rental agreement. Review your final quote and agreement before paying.

Packages and rental period

The base rental includes seven calendar days, starting on the delivery date. Pickup is scheduled seven dates later. The chosen two-hour window on that pickup date is included; a later clock time within that date does not add another rental day in this preview.

Delivery and pickup are included only within an approved standard service zone. Other addresses and extended-area deliveries may carry additional fees, disclosed before payment.

Rental extensions

Add up to four additional weeks: a maximum of 28 extra days, or 35 total rental days. Extra days cost $25 each; a full additional week costs $100. We apply the best rate, capping each seven-day block at $100.

Examples: 3 extra days = $75; 4–7 = $100; 8 = $125; 11–14 = $200; 28 = $400. The charge applies to the selected extension for this booking. Equipment availability must cover the complete rental period.

Extensions requested after a booking is accepted require a new availability and price check. Contact the team before changing a confirmed pickup date.

Advance notice and two-hour windows

Online bookings require at least 48 elapsed hours before the start of the selected delivery window, using New Jersey time (America/New_York). For a request within 48 hours, email borrowaboxnj@gmail.com to check availability.

Delivery and pickup have separate two-hour windows and addresses. Examples shown in the preview are not live availability and are not reserved. The team confirms transport capacity, equipment availability and both addresses before accepting a request.

Coverage, charges and final quote

Based in Central New Jersey, Borrow A Box delivers reusable moving boxes throughout New Jersey, with extended delivery available across the tri-state area for an additional fee. All coverage is subject to address and scheduling availability.

Both delivery and collection locations are checked. Any extended-area surcharge, collection charge, applicable taxes and requested supplies must appear in the itemized final quote before payment. A pending charge is not treated as free. Locations outside an approved zone require a custom quote.

Optional packing supplies and dollies

Each package includes one dolly. Additional dollies are priced at $25 each for this preview booking, subject to availability and the final quote. Fragile kit contents and prices, and individual supply products and prices, are not finalized. Requests for those items are quoted separately and are excluded from the displayed known subtotal.

No add-on is preselected. Requested quantities do not reserve stock until the team accepts the booking.

Payment and booking confirmation

The GitHub preview does not collect payment, reserve inventory or send automatic booking confirmations. Preparing or downloading a request does not submit it. An email draft is sent only when you send it from your email app.

Before accepting a final booking, review the package, quantities, dates, two addresses and windows, extensions, extras, fees and total. Confirmation requires availability acceptance and the applicable payment arrangement. Keep your final confirmation for delivery day.

Changes, cancellations and equipment care

The original agreement requires changes or cancellations at least 48 hours before scheduled delivery for a full refund. Cancellations within 48 hours may incur a $25 restocking fee. This cancellation rule is separate from the advance-booking requirement. The final accepted agreement determines your cancellation terms.

Reusable rental equipment remains Borrow A Box’s property. The live agreement requires an adult aged 18 or older at delivery. Return totes empty, clean and stacked in an accessible place. Use supplied labels; do not apply duct tape or other high-tack adhesives.

The live agreement lists replacement fees of $25 per large tote, $15 per medium tote and $75 per dolly. All other provisions, including liability and governing law, remain available in the original agreement; this preview is not a replacement signed contract.
